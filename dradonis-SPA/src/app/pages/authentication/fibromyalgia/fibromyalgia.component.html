THE SCIENCE

What Fibromyalgia Is

You have probably been told the tests were normal. You may have been told it was stress, or depression, or that you should try to relax.

Fibromyalgia is a chronic condition characterized by widespread musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, sleep disturbance, and cognitive difficulty. The current understanding centers on central sensitization: the nervous system’s pain processing has become amplified, so signals that should register as mild register as severe, and stimuli that should not register as painful do.

This is not imagination and it is not a low pain threshold. It is a change in how the central nervous system processes input.

The problem is that the standard workup was never designed to find what drives it, so the absence of findings gets interpreted as the absence of a problem.

schedule

Disrupted Deep Sleep

Sleep architecture is consistently altered in fibromyalgia populations, and deep sleep is where muscle repair and pain modulation happen.

science

Cellular Energy Dysfunction

Mitochondrial and cellular energy dysfunction is consistently described, alongside altered stress hormone patterns.

Low-Grade Inflammation

Evidence of ongoing low-grade inflammatory activity, which adds to the total load the nervous system is processing.
