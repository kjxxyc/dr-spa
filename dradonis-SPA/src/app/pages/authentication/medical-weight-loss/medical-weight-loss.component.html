THE APPROACH

What Medical Weight Loss Is

Medical weight loss is weight management directed by a physician, built on diagnostic testing, and using prescription therapy where it is clinically appropriate.

The distinction from a commercial program is the workup. Before any medication is prescribed, we establish what is driving the resistance, because several distinct problems produce the same outcome and they do not respond to the same treatment.

For a significant number of people, no amount of willpower overrides physiology it cannot see.

GLP-1 medications are genuinely effective. Neither addresses why your metabolism resisted in the first place, which is the part that determines what happens when you stop.
