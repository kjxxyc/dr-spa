THE APPROACH

What Functional Medicine Actually Means

Most chronic complaints get one of two responses: a medication that reduces the symptom, or a workup that finds nothing and concludes the problem is stress. Both leave the patient managing something indefinitely.

Functional medicine works in the other direction. It starts from the symptom, uses comprehensive laboratory testing to identify the systems driving it, and corrects those systems in sequence. The aim is not to suppress what you are feeling but to remove the reason you are feeling it.

There is usually a mechanism, and it is usually findable.

It is not a replacement for conventional medicine. Red-flag symptoms, structural disease, and conditions requiring specialist management need conventional evaluation, and we refer for those directly.

MEDICAL EXPERTISE

Why A Surgeon Practices Root-Cause Medicine

Dr. Adonis trained as a neurosurgeon, completing postgraduate and fellowship training in the Department of Surgery and Neurosurgery at the University of South Florida, and performed hundreds of pituitary procedures before turning to functional and regenerative medicine.

What moved him was the recognition that so much of what he saw arriving at the operating room began years earlier, in systems nobody had examined. He is certified by the Institute for Functional Medicine and board certified by the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine, has practiced in Miami since 2004, and is the author of Modern Medicine for Modern Times.
