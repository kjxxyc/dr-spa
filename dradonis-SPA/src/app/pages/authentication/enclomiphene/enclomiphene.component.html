THE MECHANISM

What Enclomiphene Is

Enclomiphene is a selective estrogen receptor modulator that increases your body’s own testosterone production.

The mechanism is upstream. Your hypothalamus and pituitary monitor circulating estrogen as a proxy for how much testosterone is around. When they detect enough, they reduce LH and FSH output, and the testicles produce less. Enclomiphene blocks estrogen receptors at the hypothalamus, so the brain reads the level as low and increases LH and FSH.

The outcome is a higher testosterone level generated by your own physiology, with the pituitary-testicular axis intact and sperm production preserved.

Enclomiphene is the isomer of clomiphene citrate responsible for the testosterone-raising effect, separated out from the isomer associated with more of the estrogenic side effects.
