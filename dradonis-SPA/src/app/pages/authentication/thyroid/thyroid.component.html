THE SCIENCE

What Thyroid Dysfunction Is

The thyroid produces hormones that set the metabolic rate of nearly every cell you have. When output falls, everything slows. When it rises, everything accelerates.

The chain matters because most thyroid problems are not the gland failing. Your thyroid mainly produces T4, which is largely inactive. T4 has to convert into T3, the form your cells actually use, and that conversion happens outside the thyroid, in the liver, kidneys, and gut.

A person can have a normal TSH, adequate T4, and still be functionally hypothyroid. That patient will be told nothing is wrong.

The most common cause of hypothyroidism is autoimmune. If your antibodies come back elevated, see our Hashimoto’s page.

Overproduction. Anxiety, racing heart, weight loss, heat intolerance, tremor, and insomnia.
