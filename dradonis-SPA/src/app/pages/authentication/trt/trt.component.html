THE SCIENCE

What TRT Is

Testosterone replacement therapy restores testosterone to a healthy physiological range in men whose own production has declined.

Testosterone regulates far more than libido. It drives muscle protein synthesis, bone density, red blood cell production, fat distribution, insulin sensitivity, mood regulation, and cognition. When it falls, the effects are systemic, which is why the symptom list looks scattered until you understand the mechanism.

Levels decline gradually from roughly the mid-thirties. That decline is normal in the statistical sense. It is not automatically acceptable.

The workout that used to take a day to recover from now takes three. The afternoon you used to push through, you no longer push through. Drive went missing at some point and you cannot name the month it happened.
