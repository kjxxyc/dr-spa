THE CLINICAL PICTURE

What Erectile Dysfunction Is

ED is the persistent difficulty achieving or maintaining an erection sufficient for satisfactory sexual activity. Occasional difficulty is normal and universal. A consistent pattern is a clinical finding.

Erectile dysfunction is rarely just an inconvenience. It is frequently the first visible sign that one of those systems is under strain, and it often shows up years before anything else does.

The penile arteries are narrower than the coronary arteries, which is why they show impairment earlier.

The causes fall into several categories, and most men have more than one running at once.
