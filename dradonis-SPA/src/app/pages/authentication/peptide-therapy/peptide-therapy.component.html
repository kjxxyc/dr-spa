THE SCIENCE

What Peptides Are & How They Work

Peptides are among the most oversold categories in wellness medicine right now, and among the most interesting. Both things are true.

There are peptides with meaningful clinical rationale used appropriately under physician supervision, and there is a large market selling them without testing, without oversight, and frequently without a clear account of what is actually being dispensed.

The difference is entirely in how it is done.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids (typically fewer than 50). Your body already synthesizes and uses thousands of them as vital signaling molecules—including insulin, growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH), and cellular repair messengers. Peptide therapy delivers specific signaling peptides to activate defined cellular receptors, offering highly targeted physiological effects.
