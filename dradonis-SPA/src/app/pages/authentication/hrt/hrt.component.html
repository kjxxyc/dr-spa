THE SCIENCE

What Hormone Replacement Therapy Is

Hormone replacement therapy restores hormones your body has stopped producing at the levels it needs.

We use bioidentical hormones, meaning the molecules are structurally identical to the hormones your body makes. Estradiol given as HRT is the same estradiol your ovaries produced.

Identical structure means the hormone binds the same receptors and follows the same metabolic pathways as your own.

That is different from older synthetic formulations, which were structurally altered compounds that behaved differently in the body and carried a different side effect profile.
