THE MECHANISM

What Hashimoto’s Is

Hashimoto’s thyroiditis is an autoimmune condition in which the immune system produces antibodies against thyroid tissue. Over time, that attack damages the gland and hormone production falls.

It is the most common cause of hypothyroidism in the United States, and it disproportionately affects women.

The thyroid is the target here. It is not the origin.

Increased intestinal permeability is repeatedly implicated in autoimmune activation, and Hashimoto’s patients frequently have digestive symptoms nobody connected to their thyroid. See our Gut Health page.
