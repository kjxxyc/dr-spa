THE SCIENCE

What Brain Health Means Here

Your brain is metabolically expensive. It represents roughly two percent of body weight and consumes about twenty percent of your energy.

That demand makes it unusually sensitive to anything affecting fuel supply, inflammation, or hormonal signaling. Which is why cognitive symptoms so often arrive as the first visible sign of a problem somewhere else entirely.

Being told it is stress is not a workup.

Reading takes twice as long because you keep going back. Names sit just out of reach. Sometimes age and stress are the correct explanation — and often they are not, which is exactly why the question is worth testing rather than assuming.

Why A Neurosurgical Background Matters in Cognitive Care

Dr. Adonis is a neurosurgeon by training. He completed postgraduate and fellowship training in the Department of Surgery and Neurosurgery at the University of South Florida and performed hundreds of pituitary procedures before moving into functional and regenerative medicine.
